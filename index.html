gmailsyncal

Appointment sync to Google Calendar

About

gmailsyncal is part of an internal office application used only inside the office's own network. Users who choose to connect their Google account get their office appointments added to their own Google Calendar. The sync is one-way: from the office application to Google Calendar.

Privacy Policy

What the application accesses

Only Google Calendar events, through the permission “View and edit events on all your calendars”. The application does not request access to your profile, email, contacts or any other Google data.

What it sends to Google Calendar

For each appointment it creates, updates or deletes one event with:

  • the date, time and duration;
  • the type of appointment;
  • the client's name and phone number.

It changes only the events it created itself. It does not read, change or delete your other events.

Storage and sharing

The access token is stored encrypted on the office's own server and is used only for this sync. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting

You can stop the sync at any time from the application (Settings → Google Calendar → Disconnect) or from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events already added stay in your calendar until you delete them.
